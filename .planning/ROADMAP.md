## Phase 1: Foundation & Style
Goal: Transition from "Cold/Tech" to "Warm/Authentic" using typography, color, and spacing.
Requirements: STYLE-01, STYLE-02, STYLE-03, PACE-01, UI-02
Success criteria:
1. All titles use Marcellus/Serif font.
2. Background color is Warm White (#FFFFFF/Bianco Carta).
3. Padding between categories increased for better rhythm.

## Phase 2: Organic UI Components
Goal: Replace iOS-style components with organic, brand-aligned alternatives.
Requirements: UI-01, UI-03, STORY-03
Success criteria:
1. Category navigation uses "pill" or "brush" style instead of glassmorphism.
2. Watercolor-style separators are implemented between sections.

## Phase 3: Storytelling & UX
Goal: Finalize the "convivial" vibe with content and imagery.
Requirements: STORY-01, STORY-02, PACE-02
Success criteria:
1. Narrative intro "Accomodatevi..." is visible.
2. Bento-style evocative images are present.
3. Menu is seamless and "warm" on mobile devices.
