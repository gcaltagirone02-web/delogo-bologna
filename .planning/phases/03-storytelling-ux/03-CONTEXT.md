# Phase 03: Storytelling & UX - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-02-20
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Finalize the menu page with narrative content, evocative Mediterranean imagery, and refined UX animations. This phase aims to transform the page from a functional list into an immersive "convivial" experience that tells the brand's story.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Narrative Introduction (STORY-01)
- **Decided**: Implement a warm welcome section before the menu entries.
- **Content (IT)**: "Cucinare per qualcuno è un atto d'amore. Benvenuti al nostro tavolo, dove i sapori dell'Egeo incontrano il calore di Bologna. Lasciatevi guidare dai profumi e dalla tradizione."
- **Content (EN)**: "Cooking for someone is an act of love. Welcome to our table, where the flavors of the Aegean meet the warmth of Bologna. Let yourself be guided by aromas and tradition."
- **Style**: Italicized, medium-sized serif text with generous leading.

### Bento Imagery (STORY-02)
- **Decided**: Use a single Bento-style grid section positioned between the title/intro and the first category.
- **Visuals**: A mix of 3-4 images showing: traditional Greek chairs (blue/white), a table with small mezze/raki, and a sun-drenched coastal scenery.
- **Source**: Unsplash images with a warm, "golden hour" color grade.

### UX Refinement & Animations (PACE-02)
- **Decided**: Apply subtle `fade-in-up` animations to category titles as they enter the viewport to create a "premium" and "living" page feel.
- **Decided**: Ensure images have lazy-loading and proper aspect-ratio boxes to prevent layout shifts on mobile.

### Claude's Discretion
- The exact layout of the Bento grid (which image is larger/portrait vs landscape).
- The precise delay and duration of entry animations.
- Final micro-spacing adjustments for mobile typography.

</decisions>
