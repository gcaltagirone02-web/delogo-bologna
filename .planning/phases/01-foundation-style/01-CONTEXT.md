# Phase 01: Foundation & Style - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-02-20
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Set the visual and atmospheric foundation for the menu page. This includes updating the background, typography, primary color applications, and layout spacing to move from a "tech-dashboard" feel to an "authentic taverna" feel.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Visual Background
- **Decided**: Use "Bianco Carta" (#FFFFFF) but with a subtle organic texture overlay (simulating high-quality paper or fine Mediterranean stone) to avoid a cold, flat digital look.

### Typography & Headers
- **Decided**: Use Marcellus (Serif) for all category <h2> titles and the main page <h1>.
- **Decided**: Align category titles centrally to mimic a physical printed menu.
- **Decided**: Color for category headers: Rosso Corallo (#D14638) as per the brief.

### Layout & Spacing
- **Decided**: Implement generous vertical spacing (large gaps) between categories (e.g., Antipasti to Insalate) to create a relaxed reading rhythm.
- **Decided**: Maintain standard Grigio Ardesia Scuro (#2F4F4F) for body text and prices to ensure high readability.

### Claude's Discretion
- The exact implementation of the background texture (CSS noise vs. SVG pattern vs. light image).
- The precise amount of padding/margin to achieve the "relaxed" Pace-01 requirement.

</decisions>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas
- The goal is to make the page feel "materica" (tactile/physical).
- References: Classic physical taverna menus, watercolor-based art.

</specifics>
